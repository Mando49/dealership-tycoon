# Dealership Tycoon — Vela Motors Las Vegas

Run the floor at a luxury car dealership just off the Las Vegas Strip.

- Match buyers to the right car and budget
- Upsell window tint and paint protection film after every sale
- Seat service customers in the lounge and keep them happy
- Restock at auction and grow the store with upgrades

Vela Motors and its models are fictional.
